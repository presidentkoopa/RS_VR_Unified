merge of Hands, Holsters, HardPoints, Grabbing, Gestures, Distance Grabbing, Model-Frame Reloading, Headshots, WeaponWheelz

meant to serve as a centralized VR_Doom interface 


- Posable VR Hands with 20 + poses

- Reach-to-grab and distance based grab-flick, extensive options

- up to 8 holsters for your weapons, grab and holster with either hand

- three hardpoints for each hand allows holding one weapon and mounting three others for a gesture-based quick-fire system

- a modular and customizable weapon wheel tracking weapon stats and features across over a dozen of Doom's most popular and technically complex mods

- an optional second selectale inventory wheel

- a universal headshot system built to take advantage of UZDXREMAs location-based damage system

- universal reloading system for any weapon mod, provided it has 3d models (see my ModelSwapper for unviersal 3d Doom weapons for any mod)

much of the mod is still WIP but it is functional and playable
